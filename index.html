About NEXKO

One partner for the ground, the grid and the people who keep it running.

NEXKO Renewable Solutions is an engineering and project services company based in Pune. We work alongside developers, EPC contractors and plant owners across wind, solar and power distribution.

Our crews handle the physical work: pouring turbine foundations, stringing 220kV and 33kV lines, installing solar plants, building access roads, maintaining turbines. We also supply the trained people those projects need. Safety and schedule come first on every site.
