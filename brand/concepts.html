NEXKO logo concepts, round two

Abstract, premium and built around energy. Each concept is shown on white, on dark, over a photo, and as an app icon and favicon.

1Flux

Three swept blades turning around an open centre, like a rotor caught in motion. It's clearly wind energy without drawing a turbine, and the sunrise gradient adds heat and power. Best suited to a flagship brand.

2Horizon

A rising sun over layered lines that suggest wind currents, power lines and roads. It sums up what the company does, energy above and infrastructure below, in one calm, premium emblem.

3Current

An N drawn as four parallel lines of current, like conductors on a 33kV line or streams of wind. It's the most technical and corporate of the three, and the letter is built into it.
